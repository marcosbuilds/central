# Public study material renders

These PNGs are rasterized page renders of the two public study PDFs supplied
for the Peace learning area. The site serves the renders through a visual
reader and does not publish the source PDF files.

- `peace/` contains the pages from the A Peace manual.
- `civil/` contains the pages from the Material Civil apostila.

The reader adds `Cache-Control: no-store` at the local preview boundary,
disables the ordinary download affordance, and uses a visible watermark. Those
are deterrents only: a browser cannot prevent screenshots, OCR, developer
tools, or a person manually sending visible content to another service.
